Jorge Campos, Campos, Jorge Campos Navarrete
I was born in 1966 and am 170cm tall
I was awarded an honorary doctorate by Thompson Rivers University in 2023
I am a Copa América runner up
I have won my country's top-flight league with two different clubs
I am a FIFA Confederations Cup winner and a member of the FIFA Century Club, with 129 international caps
I have played alongside Rafa Márquez, Pável Pardo, and Cobi Jones
I once played two matches on the same day: one for my country and one for my club
I won the Liga MX Golden Glove five seasons in a row, from 1990–91 to 1994–95
Despite playing primarily as a goalkeeper, I scored 38 career goals
I was famous for my brightly coloured, self-designed kits, and started in goal for Mexico at the 1994 and 1998 FIFA World Cups
